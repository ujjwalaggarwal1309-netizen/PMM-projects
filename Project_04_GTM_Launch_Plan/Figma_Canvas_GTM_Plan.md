# Figma Canvas: Go-To-Market Launch Plan

## ðŸŒ Section 1: Market Context

**The Market Opportunity:**
B2B marketing teams have increased their content velocity by 300% over the last two years to feed demanding social channels. However, these teams are heavily bottlenecked by limited design resources. 

**The Current Gap:**
Canva is fast but lacks strict brand governance. Marketing teams using Canva often dilute the company's visual identity. Standard Figma has perfect governance but a terrifying learning curve for marketers. When marketers try to use standard Figma, they accidentally break auto-layout components and frustrate the core design team. 

**The Timing Rationale:**
As marketing budgets tighten heading into 2027, companies want to consolidate their software stacks. They want to eliminate rogue Canva subscriptions. Launching "Figma Canvas" now allows companies to keep all visual assets inside the Figma ecosystem while giving marketing teams a simplified, locked-down tool to move fast.

**The Persona Profile:**
Our primary buyer is a Content Marketing Manager at a Series A or B B2B SaaS company. They are responsible for publishing three blog posts and ten social media assets every week. They do not know how to use vector tools, and they do not want to learn.

## ðŸ“¢ Section 3: Positioning and Messaging

**The Core Positioning Statement:**
For fast-moving content marketing teams who are bottlenecked by limited design resources, Figma Canvas is the lightweight asset creation tool that guarantees brand consistency without the learning curve. Unlike Canva, it natively syncs with your product team's core Figma design system.

**The Hero Headline:**
> *"Stop waiting for assets. Start publishing."*

**The 3 Key Messages:**
**1. No design degree required.** Drag, drop, and publish. You get the power of Figma's rendering engine with a simplified interface built specifically for marketers.
**2. Locked-in brand guardrails.** Designers control the master templates. Marketers only control the copy and the images. Nobody can accidentally break the brand guidelines again.
**3. One unified ecosystem.** Stop paying for rogue third-party design subscriptions. Keep all your company's visual assets secure inside one platform.

## ðŸš€ Section 4: Launch Channels

**1. In-App Cross-Sell to Design Admins (Primary Channel)**
*   **Why it reaches the ICP:** The Head of Design holds the Figma budget and acutely feels the pain of repetitive marketing requests. They are highly incentivized to solve this problem.
*   **Content:** An in-app modal and targeted email sequence showing how much time their design team spends on "text tweaks" for marketing.
*   **The CTA:** "Give Marketing Their Own Workspace."
*   **Success Metric:** 20% open rate on the email sequence and 500 immediate seat upgrades.

**2. B2B Creator Campaign on LinkedIn**
*   **Why it reaches the ICP:** B2B marketing managers live on LinkedIn to study campaign trends. 
*   **Content:** We will sponsor top marketing creators to post a 60-second screen recording. The video will show them spinning up a full LinkedIn carousel campaign using Canvas in under 5 minutes.
*   **The CTA:** "Try Figma Canvas for free."
*   **Success Metric:** 1.5 million organic impressions and 10,000 click-throughs to the landing page.

**3. Template-Led SEO (Product-Led Growth)**
*   **Why it reaches the ICP:** Marketers constantly search Google for "Free B2B Social Media Templates."
*   **Content:** We will launch a library of 50+ high-converting B2B templates that index heavily on search. To edit the template, the user is forced to create a free Figma Canvas account.
*   **The CTA:** "Edit this template in Canvas."
*   **Success Metric:** Top 3 Google ranking for "B2B social templates" within 60 days.
